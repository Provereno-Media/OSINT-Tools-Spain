# OSINT-Tools-Spain

<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/9/9a/Flag_of_Spain.svg" alt="Spain flag" width="96" />

A list of OSINT resources and tools for investigations related to Spain. Most resources are in Spanish; some are also available in English, Catalan, Basque, or other languages. Entries describe only their free public features; paid reports and restricted records are excluded.

**Last Updated:** 27 September 2026  
**Languages:** Spanish, English, Catalan, Basque; other regional languages on selected sites.  
**Comments:** Free access does not imply unrestricted personal-data access. The national cadastre excludes the Basque Country and Navarre; use the separate regional services below. Some free services require registration or official electronic identification.

## Open Data portals
- [datos.gob.es](https://datos.gob.es/en/) — Official Spanish government catalogue of reusable public datasets; Spanish/English; free.
- [INE Open Data](https://www.ine.es/datosabiertos/) — Official statistics and reusable datasets from the National Statistics Institute; Spanish; free.
- [BOE Search](https://www.boe.es/buscar/) — Official national gazette search for legislation, announcements, notifications and historical issues; Spanish; free.
- [CENDOJ Case Law Search](https://www.poderjudicial.es/search/indexAN.jsp) — Official judicial-decision search; Spanish; free public consultation, with possible anti-bulk-download checks.
- [Banco de España Statistics](https://www.bde.es/wbe/en/estadisticas/) — Official economic, financial and monetary statistics; English/Spanish; free.

## Legal Entities
- [BORME](https://www.boe.es/diario_borme/) — Official Mercantile Registry Gazette with company notices and corporate acts; Spanish; free public issues, not a complete free company extract.
- [CNMV Entity Search](https://www.cnmv.es/portal/consultas/busquedaporentidad?lang=es) — Official securities-regulator filings and registered financial-market entities; Spanish; free.
- [National Associations Search](https://sede.interior.gob.es/portal/sede/asociaciones) — Official Ministry of the Interior search by association name; Spanish; free public search, not a complete authenticated extract.
- [EU VIES](https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/vies/) — European Commission validation of Spanish and other EU VAT numbers; multilingual; free.
- [GLEIF LEI Search](https://search.gleif.org/) — Third-party global index of entities assigned an LEI, filterable for Spain; English; free, not comprehensive company coverage.

## Maps
- [Spanish Cadastre](https://www.sedecatastro.gob.es/) — Official property search and cadastral map; Spanish; free public non-protected data, excluding the Basque Country and Navarre; ownership and protected values are not public.
- [SIGPAC Viewer](https://sigpac.mapa.gob.es/fega/visor/) — Official agricultural parcel, land-use and aerial-image viewer; Spanish; free.
- [IBERPIX](https://www.ign.es/iberpix) — Official IGN topographic maps, geocoding and aerial-image viewer; Spanish/English interface elements; free.
- [Geoportal IDEE](https://www.idee.es/) — Official Spanish spatial-data infrastructure catalogue and access to regional mapping services; Spanish/English and regional languages; free.
- [PNOA Map and Orthophoto Viewers](https://pnoa.ign.es/pnoa-imagen/visualizadores-y-servicios-web) — Official historical and current aerial-photography comparison tools; Spanish; free.

### Basque Country
The three Basque historical territories operate separate foral cadastral services; geoEuskadi provides complementary regional maps. Public parcel data does not establish legal ownership.
- [Álava Cadastre](https://web.araba.eus/es/hacienda/catastro) — Official Álava parcel, building and physical-property data viewer; Spanish/Basque; free public maps, while protected values require owner authentication.
- [Bizkaia Cadastre](https://www.bizkaia.eus/es/catastro-de-bizkaia) — Official Bizkaia cadastral viewer and parcel references; Spanish/Basque; free public map and reference search, with restricted owner-specific data.
- [Gipuzkoa Parcel Maps](https://www.gipuzkoa.eus/es/web/ogasuna/catastro/herramientas-gestion/planos-parcelarios) — Official Gipuzkoa urban and rural parcel plans, viewable and downloadable; Spanish/Basque; free.
- [geoEuskadi](https://www.geo.euskadi.eus/inicio/) — Official Basque regional map viewer with parcel search and historical orthophotos; Spanish/Basque; free.

### Navarre
Navarre's Territorial Wealth Registry and IDENA/SITNA provide separate cadastral and geographic coverage; use public datasets lawfully and verify any ownership claim independently.
- [Navarre Territorial Wealth Registry](https://catastro.navarra.es/navegar/) — Official Navarre cadastral map with parcel, address and geographic searches; Spanish/Basque/English interface elements; free public viewer.
- [Navarre Cadastral Downloads](https://catastro.navarra.es/descargas/) — Official municipality-level parcel geometry and public alphanumeric data, including SHP/ASCII files; Spanish/Basque/English/French; free under CC BY 4.0.
- [Navarre Geoportal Viewers](https://geoportal.navarra.es/es/visores) — Official IDENA/SITNA map, historical orthophoto comparison and LiDAR viewers; Spanish; free.

## Vehicles
### Land
- [DGT Environmental Badge Lookup](https://sede.dgt.gob.es/es/vehiculos/informacion-de-vehiculos/distintivo-ambiental/) — Official vehicle-emissions class by Spanish plate; Spanish; free lookup without login, while a physical badge costs money.
- [DGT Reduced Vehicle Report](https://www.dgt.es/nuestros-servicios/tu-vehiculo/tus-vehiculos/informe-de-un-vehiculo/) — Official first-registration date and transfer/circulation alerts by plate; Spanish; free reduced report, with electronic identification potentially required; detailed reports are paid and excluded.
### Maritime
- [Equasis](https://www.equasis.org/) — International ship and maritime-safety records for Spain-linked vessels; English; free, ⚠️ registration required.
- [VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com/vessels) — Third-party ship specifications and visible AIS positions, searchable by vessel identifiers; English; free basic search, with advanced features limited.
### Aviation
- [AESA Aircraft Register](https://www.seguridadaerea.gob.es/es/ambitos/aeronaves/registro-de-matriculas-de-aeronaves-civiles/registro-de-matriculas) — Official downloadable list of Spanish-registered civil aircraft; Spanish; free public list, while ownership certificates are fee-based and excluded.
- [Flightradar24 Aviation Database](https://www.flightradar24.com/data) — Third-party aircraft, airport and flight discovery for Spain; English; free basic search, with advanced history limited.

## People
- [Congress Member Search](https://www.congreso.es/busqueda-de-diputados) — Official current and historical Spanish deputies and parliamentary profiles; Spanish; free.
- [Senate Member Search](https://www.senado.es/web/composicionorganizacion/senadores/composicionsenado/senadoresenactivo/index.html) — Official active senators searchable by name and origin; Spanish; free.
- [Senior Officials' Asset Declarations](https://transparencia.gob.es/publicidad-activa/por-materias/altos-cargos/declaraciones-bienes-derechos) — Official published declarations for central-government senior officials, with location data omitted; Spanish; free.
- [State Administration Directory](https://administracion.gob.es/espanaadmon/quienesquien/organizacionage) — Official ministry structures and links to public office directories; Spanish; free; some overview text may be older than linked directories.
- [Páginas Amarillas](https://www.paginasamarillas.es/) — Third-party business and professional contact directory by name or locality; Spanish; free public search, not a residential register.

## Public procurement
- [Ministry of Finance Tender Open Data](https://www.hacienda.gob.es/es-ES/GobiernoAbierto/Datos%20Abiertos/Paginas/licitaciones_plataforma_contratacion.aspx) — Official reusable Spanish tender, minor-contract and contracting-authority datasets; Spanish; free.
- [Catalonia Public Procurement Platform](https://contractaciopublica.cat/ca/inici) — Official Catalan tender and award notices searchable by file; Catalan/Spanish; free public search.
- [Madrid Public Contract Search](http://contratos-publicos.comunidad.madrid/contratos) — Official Madrid regional tenders and awards with filters and CSV export; Spanish; free public search.
- [TED](https://ted.europa.eu/en/search/result) — Official EU tender notices, including Spanish authorities and contractors; multilingual; free public search.
- [Plataforma PYME Tender Finder](https://plataformapyme.es/es-es/herramientas-digitales/Paginas/buscador_licitaciones.aspx) — Official SME-oriented discovery of open and awarded tenders; Spanish; free public information.

## WHOIS
- [Dominios.es](https://www.dominios.es/es) — Official .es registry with public domain-availability search; Spanish; free; the separate port-43 WHOIS service requires prior accreditation and is not an open lookup.
- [ICANN Lookup](https://lookup.icann.org/es) — Official RDAP registration-data lookup for supported domains and Internet resources; Spanish/English; free; some registrant data is non-public.
- [RIPE Database](https://apps.db.ripe.net/db-web-ui/query) — Official regional Internet Registry search for IP ranges, networks and ASNs; English; free; not a domain-owner registry.
- [crt.sh](https://crt.sh/) — Third-party certificate-transparency search for domain-linked certificates and hostnames; English; free; not proof of domain ownership.
- [MxToolbox DNS Lookup](https://mxtoolbox.com/DNSLookup.aspx) — Third-party DNS-record and mail-infrastructure lookup; English; free basic search; not proof of domain ownership.
